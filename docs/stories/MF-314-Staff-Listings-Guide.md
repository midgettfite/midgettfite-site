# Keeping the Website Listings Current

**Midgett & Fite — one-page guide for the office**

Sign in at **app.pagescms.org** with your work email. You'll get a sign-in link by email; there's no password.
Open **Property Listings**. Every change you save goes live on midgettfite.com in about a minute.

---

## Add a listing

1. Press **Add an entry**.
2. **Listing Type**: For Rent or For Sale, Residential or Commercial.
3. **Status**: *Available*.
4. **Street Address**: write the street type the way the Vacancies tab has it ("209 Burdock **St**", not "209 Burdock"). Ivy matches listings to the Vacancies tab by address.
5. Fill in price, bedrooms, bathrooms, square feet and the description.
6. **Photos**: upload them, then drag the best one to the **top**. The first photo is the cover on the website and the picture in Ivy's emails.
7. Press **Save**.

## Change the price

1. Open the listing, change **Price**.
2. Tick **Show "Price Update!" badge** if you want the badge.
3. **Save**.

## Mark it leased or sold

1. Open the listing, set **Status** to *Sold or Leased*. (Use *Under Contract* while a sale is pending.)
2. **Save**. It moves to the bottom of the page with a LEASED! or SOLD! badge, and the price is hidden.

## Take it down completely

Open the listing, open the **⋯** menu, choose **Delete**, and confirm.

---

## If something looks wrong

- **Nothing is ever lost.** Every save is kept, so any change, including a delete, can be put back.
- Give it a minute after saving, then refresh the page.
- Still wrong? Email **steve@volunteer-ai.com** with the address of the listing.
