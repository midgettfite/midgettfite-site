# Changing the Team, Testimonials, Office Details and Home Page

**Midgett & Fite: a page for the staff guide. DRAFT, not handed out yet (MF-315).**

Sign in at **app.pagescms.org** the same way you do for listings. Under **Property Listings** there are four more
forms. Change what you need, press **Save**, and it's on midgettfite.com in about 2 minutes.

---

## Team

- **Add someone:** press the add button under the list. Fill in all four boxes: name, job title, photo, and a few words about them.
- **Remove someone:** press the delete button on their row, then **Save**.
- **Change the order:** drag a person up or down, then **Save**. The page shows them in the same order as the list.
- **Photo:** square, head and shoulders, under 3.4 MB. A photo straight from a phone is usually too big: email it to yourself, choose **Medium**, and use that copy.

## Testimonials

- **Add one:** paste what the client said and type their name the way it should show, like *Levi H.* Leave off the quotation marks; the website adds them.
- **Order:** drag it up or down. **Remove:** delete its row. Then **Save**.
- The reviews with photos on the **Home page** are a separate list, in the Home Page form. To take a client off the website, remove them in **both** places.

## Office Details

- Change the phone number, email or address **once** here and it changes on every page.
- **Tell Steve before you change the phone number or the address.** Ivy, the text messages, the map link, the Company History page and where the contact form is delivered are set up separately.
- A listing that gives the phone number in its description has to be changed in that listing.
- **Office Hours** is blank now, so no hours show. Type them in and they appear on Contact Us.

## Home Page

- **Welcome Text:** the sentence under *Get In Touch* on the Home page. (Contact Us has its own copy; ask Steve.)
- **Slideshow Photos:** wide photos, under 3.4 MB. Drag to change the order.
- **Caption:** say what the photo shows. It isn't printed on the photo; it's read aloud to visitors who can't see it.
- **Show the whole photo on phones:** turn it on for a photo that has words on it.
- **Reviews on the Home Page:** type the quotation marks yourself here.

---

## If something looks wrong

- **Not saved yet?** Leave the form without saving, and nothing changes.
- **Already saved?** Open the form again and put it back the way it was. The clock button shows who changed it and when.
- **Removed something by mistake?** Type it in again, or ask Steve. Every save is kept, so it can always be put back.
- **Saved, but it isn't on the website after 5 minutes?** Open the form and check that entry: a box is empty, or its photo was deleted, renamed, or isn't a JPG, PNG or WebP. An entry with a problem is left off the page until it's fixed.
- **Don't delete or rename pictures in the Media list.** A page may be using them.
- **If nothing is updating, listings included,** one of these forms has a problem that holds up the whole website (it stays as it was). Undo your last change there, or email **steve@volunteer-ai.com**.
