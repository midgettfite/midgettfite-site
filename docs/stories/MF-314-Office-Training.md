# MF-314 — Train the office on the listings editor

**Kind:** story · **Status:** DRAFT · **Source:** `docs/MF-Website-Migration-Plan.md` §6 D4
**Related:** staff editor invites (MF-311), go-live (MF-312)
**Part of:** Website

**As** office staff,
**I want** to be shown once how to add, change and take down a listing, with a one-page sheet to keep,
**so that** I can keep the website current without help.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Each person adds a test listing with photos, changes its price (the "Price Update!" badge shows), marks it
   leased, and deletes it, on their own, during the session.
2. A one-page cheat sheet (PDF) covering those four tasks, plus what to do if something looks wrong.
3. Each person knows that every change is kept and can be undone, so a mistake isn't a disaster.
4. Done before go-live, so the office can keep listings current from the first day.

## Decision needed from Dan
| Question | Recommended default |
|---|---|
| In person at the office, or a screen-share? | **In person**, on the office computers, about 30 minutes. |
| Who attends? | Everyone invited in MF-311. |
| Who owns keeping listings current after go-live? | No default; one named person works best. |
